UX evaluation · packages/gov-store/custom-requests · 14 Oct 2026

The request workflow is careful underneath. On screen, people can't see their own request.

I reviewed all ten screens the package renders (catalog, draft basket, my requests, approvals, the decision page, the issue queue, issue items, the issue register and its record, request policies) plus the notices, navbar basket, substitution dialog and timeline. I walked the full journey on the seeded office as an employee, a primary approver and a storekeeper, measured contrast and target sizes in the browser, and read every refusal path in the services. Each screen is scored against ten heuristics weighted for Bangla use and for the four people who do this work.

Human-centred score

33/ 100

Independent approvals, stock reservation, locking, substitution rules and goods issue documents are solid. What fails is feedback: the requester has no page for their own request, refusals end on bare error pages, and statuses don't say what happens next. Most fixes are views, controllers and one new page. The services stay.

Who does this work

Employee · requests

Nasrin, office assistant

Asks for toner, paper or a laptop a few times a month, usually on a shared PC, often in Bangla.

  • Needs: find it fast, ask once, know when to collect.
  • Fears: asking wrong and being refused without a reason.
Approver · primary or final

Kamal, section head

Decides 5 to 20 requests a week between meetings, sometimes on a phone.

  • Needs: stock, stage and reason at a glance.
  • Fears: approving what the store can't give.
Storekeeper · issues

Rafiq, store assistant

Issues from a counter with a barcode scanner, a queue of people and a paper register.

  • Needs: the oldest first, issue all in one step.
  • Fears: wrong stock count, an audit question later.
National administrator · rules

Ahsan, national ICT officer

Sets who approves what for every office at once, a few times a year and after audits.

  • Needs: see the effect of a rule before saving it.
  • Fears: a rule that lets big orders through unseen, everywhere.

Keep these

  • Independent approval. Nobody approves their own request, and the two stages need two different people.
  • Stock is reserved on approval under row locks, so two approvals can't promise the same box.
  • Safe substitution. Same category, known cost, never dearer than what was approved.
  • Goods issue documents and returns that go through the store's normal receipt and posting.
  • Quantity autosave in the basket, and Add without leaving the catalog.
  • One queue definition (RequestQueues) behind each page and its menu badge; durable notice outbox; escalation after 3 weekdays.

The journey today

Nasrin needs a toner cartridge and two reams of paper. Kamal approves, Rafiq issues. The quotes are illustrative, written from what each screen shows.

1 · FindNasrin

"The quick buttons say Laptop, Mouse, Chair. Our toner is listed in Bangla. I scroll past three boxes first."

2 · DescribeNasrin

"Purpose, justification, cost centre, request type… for one toner? The form was there even when my basket was empty."

3 · SendNasrin

"'Service Request(s) [SR-41, SR-42] submitted!' I asked once. Why are there two?"

4 · WaitNasrin

"It says Approved. By whom? Is the paper approved too? When do I collect it? I can't click the number."

5 · DecideKamal

"Do we even have 12 reams? Am I the last approver? If I cut it, how will she know why?"

6 · IssueRafiq

"I type 1, then 2… zero is the default. The big red Force Close is right under my button."

7 · ReceiveNasrin

"Confirm receipt is a small green button inside the status column. Did it work?"

Principles for the redesign

One request, one story.

The person who asked can open it and see where it is, who has it and what happens next.

Errors speak and keep your input.

Every refusal is a sentence where the problem is. No bare error pages.

Decide with the facts at hand.

Stock, stage, the rule's reason and the age sit beside every decision.

The usual case is one step.

Issue everything approved, approve as asked, reorder the same items.

Irreversible steps show their reach.

Closing, declining and rule changes say who and what they affect, and need a reason.

One vocabulary, two languages.

চাহিদাপত্র everywhere in Bangla; whole sentences, never stitched fragments.
