UX evaluation · packages/gov-store/office-membership · 14 Oct 2026

Office Membership is safe underneath. On screen, nobody can tell what happens next.

I reviewed every screen the package renders: My office memberships, Staff management, the Emergency override console, the membership notices and the navbar office chip. I also traced its five workflows end to end: join with an office code, add with a personal code, hand over a role, leave and transfer, and the emergency override. Each screen was scored against ten usability heuristics, weighted for Bangla use and for the three people who do this work: front-line staff, office administrators and national ICT officers.

Human-centred score

35/ 100

The rules engine, locking and audit trail are solid. What fails is feedback: blank statuses, error pages with no words, and a transfer that disappears after one click. Most fixes are presentation and controller changes. The services stay as they are.

Keep these

  • The clearance engine. Pluggable rules that count real holdings (assets, accessories, components, licence seats, nested assets) and roles.
  • Handovers need consent. A role moves only when the colleague accepts, inside a locked transaction.
  • Clearance is checked three times: on request, at sign-off and at claim, so a stale screen can't release someone who still holds stock.
  • The office chip. Search, recent offices, aria-current, and it never renders the full office list.
  • Notices outbox and override audit log. Mail survives outages; every override is on record with its reason.
  • Key parity. en-US and bn-BD each have 266 keys.

Principles for the redesign

Say what's next, and who does it.

Every status comes with its next step and the name of the person it waits on.

Errors speak, and keep your input.

Every refusal is a sentence under the field it concerns. No bare error pages.

Fix it where you see it.

A held laptop links to the return. A role links to its handover. A blocked button says why.

Risky actions show their reach.

Before an override, release or transfer, show exactly who and what it changes.

Whole sentences, both languages.

No stitched fragments. বাংলা reads as written, not translated.
