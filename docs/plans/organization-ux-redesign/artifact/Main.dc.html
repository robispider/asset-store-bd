UX evaluation · packages/gov-store/organization · 14 Oct 2026

The Organization package works. It just asks people to think like the database.

I reviewed all 11 Blade screens: registry, create, onboard, office hub, lifecycle, configuration, ICT jurisdictions, ministry admins, directory import, and the two pages staff see before go-live. Each was checked against ten usability heuristics, with extra weight on Bangla use and on the three groups who do this work: area ICT officers, office administrators and front-line staff.

Human-centred score

42 / 100

The data model and safety rails are solid. Language, feedback and flow are where people get lost. Most fixes are presentation-only and can ship without touching the services.

What already works (keep it)

The readiness model

"An office goes live when 4 things are true" is the right mental model. The redesign puts it at the centre.

Live duplicate check

Warns before a second office is created in the same area and ministry. It needs to link to the match, though.

Scoped area search

ICT officers only see areas inside their jurisdiction, so they can't make the wrong choice at all.

Safe lifecycle changes

Typed confirmation, a required reason and expected-status concurrency guards are solid foundations.

Bilingual groundwork

en-US and bn-BD both have 409 keys, and the shared --gs tokens and <x-gs::*> kit cover 6 themes in both modes.

Per-office audit trail

Every change is logged. It just needs to be written as sentences instead of event codes.

Findings, most severe first

Each one names the file it comes from and the fix the redesign applies.

CriticalError recovery

Form errors are invisible, and a failed save wipes choices

None of the 11 views uses @error or $errors. The ministry, parent and area selects never call old(), so after a validation failure the user re-enters everything without being told what was wrong.

provisioning/create.blade.php · onboard.blade.php · hub.blade.php

Fix: use <x-gs::form-row>, which already renders $errors under each field, add an error summary at the top, and keep every value with old().

CriticalLanguage

Bangla users hit English walls mid-task

About 40 strings skip translation: the hub role labels, "Operational Readiness Checklist", "Needs: ✓ Primary", "View Hub", "Executed by", both readiness pages in full, and the select2 placeholders. "Type CHANGE to confirm" makes people type an English word.

hub.blade.php · readiness/*.blade.php · jurisdictions.blade.php · lifecycle.blade.php

Fix: every string goes through orglabel, and confirmation uses a checkbox or the office's own name.

HighTheming · consistency

The package bypasses the GovStore theming kit

The theming package ships 6 themes, light and dark, variants and an <x-gs::*> kit. The organisation views use only <x-gs::table>. Everything else is AdminLTE boxes, label-* badges and 88 one-off org-inline-* classes, repainted by 61 !important declarations in organization.css. Office status ignores the status_style variant, and density and radius never apply.

organization/src/resources/css/organization.css · every view

Fix: rebuild on the kit: page-header, status-badge (5 new office states), status-tabs, form-row, alert, empty-state and timeline, plus the new readiness, role-slot, action-row, choice-card and trail components.

HighLanguage · status

Status words describe the database, not the work

"Unconfigured → Provisioned → Configured → Operational" doesn't say what to do next. "Configured" really means "has an admin, roles still missing". The configuration page prints strtoupper(lifecycle_status).

OfficeReadinessService.php:51 · configuration/index.blade.php

Fix: Not onboarded · Needs administrator · Setting up · Live · Paused, each shown with its next step.

HighTrust

The waiting page tells staff something untrue

"Office Administrator Designated: Completed" is hard-coded no matter what the real state is. The unassigned page tells staff to fix things "inside Snipe-IT", a product name they've never seen.

readiness/waiting.blade.php · readiness/unassigned.blade.php

Fix: build the page from the readiness checklist, name a real person to contact, and give one clear thing to ask for.

HighControl

Risky actions sit in the open, unexplained

The lifecycle form is always visible under the hub tabs. One dropdown mixes Suspend, Resume and Relocate, and the area field shows even when it doesn't apply. Every revoke is a full-width red button guarded only by a browser confirm(), with no undo.

provisioning/lifecycle.blade.php · jurisdictions.blade.php · company-admins.blade.php

Fix: put each action under Settings with its own explained dialog, use quiet row menus, and offer an undo toast.

HighFlow

"Create" or "Onboard" is a technical decision made too early

Two buttons side by side make people know, before they start, whether a legacy location record exists. A wrong guess means a duplicate office or a dead end.

provisioning/index.blade.php:55–62

Fix: one "Add office" button. Search existing locations first, then offer "create new".

MediumClarity

Read-only fields dressed up as inputs

In the hub, ministry, parent and area show as disabled selects with no reason and no way to change them. The create form shows a "Delegate admin" select that is always disabled.

hub.blade.php:45–75 · create.blade.php:60–66

Fix: show them as plain text with "Why can't I change this?" and a link to the right action.

MediumConsistency

Three checklists, three vocabularies

The readiness checklist is built three times (hub Roles tab, configuration page, waiting page), labelled Ready/Missing, Configured/Missing and Assigned/Awaiting setup. None of the items links to its fix.

hub.blade.php · configuration/index.blade.php · readiness/waiting.blade.php

Fix: one shared readiness component where every item is a button to its fix.

MediumLanguage

Jargon and internals leak into the UI

"Spatial-First Principle", the table name gov_geo_areas, raw hierarchy codes, "Snipe-IT's core catalog", "Apply office transition", "Starter catalog", "Executed by".

orglabel.php · hub.blade.php (Geography tab)

Fix: rewrite in plain language and move IDs behind "Technical details".

MediumEfficiency

The registry is slow for the people who use it daily

The KPI tiles aren't filters. Filters wait for a Filter click, and the status filter has no "Not onboarded" option. Actions are 22px btn-xs buttons, rows aren't clickable, and what's missing is shown only as coloured ✓/✗ marks.

provisioning/index.blade.php

Fix: status tabs with counts, instant filters, the whole row as a link, and a "Next step" column.

MediumAccessibility

Accessibility basics are missing

There are no aria attributes, screen readers announce decorative icons, some signals rely on colour alone, tables have no captions, focus states aren't designed, and most tap targets are under 44px.

all views · organization.css

Fix: build WCAG 2.2 AA into the shared components (Foundations board).

Five principles for the redesign

01Tell people what's next

Every screen answers "what do I do now?" before "what is the data?".

02Two languages, one experience

Every word, number and confirmation works the same in বাংলা as in English.

03Fix it where you see it

A missing role, an unverified area, an empty staff list: each comes with its own button, right there.

04Make risky actions calm

Explain consequences in plain words, ask once, and offer undo where possible.

05Respect the daily user

Instant filters, keyboard-friendly, dense where it helps, never cluttered.
